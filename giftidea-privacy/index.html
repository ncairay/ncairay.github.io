GiftIdea - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

GiftIdea does not collect any personally identifiable information or analytics data. All gift searches are processed anonymously. We do not require account registration.

2. How We Use Information

The preferences you select (such as relationship, interests, and budget) are sent through our server to a third-party AI service only to generate gift suggestions. You are asked for consent before this happens. We do not store or retain them on our servers, and they cannot be used to identify individual users. The AI service processes them only to generate suggestions, under its own data policy..

3. Third-Party Services

GiftIdea uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenRouter API - AI-powered gift recommendations
  • Involve Asia - affiliate network for shopping links to partner merchants
  • Google Search - general product search links

4. Affiliate Links

Some shopping links in the app are affiliate links. We may earn a commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links never influence our gift recommendations: the AI chooses gifts without knowing which merchants we work with.

When you tap a shopping link, the merchant's website opens in your browser. The merchant and the affiliate network may use cookies to record the purchase. Their own privacy policies apply to anything you do on their websites.

5. App Configuration

When the app starts, it downloads small configuration files (such as the list of partner merchants and current offers) from our website. No personal data is sent in these requests.

6. Data Storage

Search history and saved gifts are stored locally on your device only. We do not store any user data on our servers.

7. Children's Privacy

GiftIdea is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

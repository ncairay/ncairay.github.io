GiftIdea - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

GiftIdea does not collect any personally identifiable information or analytics data. All gift searches are processed anonymously. We do not require account registration.

2. How We Use Information

The preferences you select (such as relationship, interests, and budget) are sent to a third-party AI service only to generate gift suggestions. They are not stored or retained, and cannot be used to identify individual users.

3. Third-Party Services

GiftIdea uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenRouter API - AI-powered gift recommendations
  • Affiliate links (Amazon, Klook) - purchasing recommendations

Some links in the app are affiliate links. These do not affect our gift recommendations.

4. Data Storage

Search history and saved gifts are stored locally on your device only. We do not store any user data on our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

GiftIdea is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
